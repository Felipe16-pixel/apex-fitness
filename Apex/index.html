FAQ

Do I need gym equipment?
+

No equipment required for most programs. We offer bodyweight, home gym, and full gym variations for every workout. Select your setup when you sign up.

Can I cancel anytime?
+

Absolutely. Cancel your subscription anytime with no penalties. We believe in earning your membership every month through results, not contracts.

How is APEX different from YouTube workouts?
+

APEX delivers periodized, progressive programs built around your goals — not random videos. Our coaches track your progress, adapt your plan, and keep you accountable throughout.

Is there a free trial?
+

Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card needed to start. Experience full access to your chosen plan before committing.

What if I'm a complete beginner?
+

APEX welcomes all levels. Our onboarding assessment places you in the right program, and beginner-specific tracks ensure safe, effective progress from day one.

Do you offer nutrition guidance?
+

Yes. All plans include access to our nutrition library. Pro and Elite members get personalized meal planning from certified sports nutritionists.
